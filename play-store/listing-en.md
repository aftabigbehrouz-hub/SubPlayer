## Full description  (2231 of 4000 characters)

```
Learn German or English with the videos you actually want to watch.

SubPlayer writes subtitles for your own videos right on your phone, explains every word you tap and brings the words you save back right before you would forget them.

★ SUBTITLES FOR YOUR OWN VIDEOS
• Offline speech recognition (OpenAI Whisper) on your phone: no upload, no internet needed after a one-time model download
• German and English
• Optional "Online" mode with your own Groq or OpenAI key: fastest and most accurate
• Already have subtitles? Load .srt or .vtt files, and save or share the subtitles you made

★ TAP ANY WORD
• Pause and tap: base form, article, IPA, meanings with example sentences
• German words: English and Persian; English words: definitions, German and Persian
• Offline dictionaries built from Wiktionary (about 205,000 German and 131,000 English entries)
• Hear the word, slowly too; tap two words for a phrase translation, or translate the whole sentence
• Subtitle lines are joined into the complete sentence

★ REPEAT LIKE VLC
• A-B loop, loop the current line, pause after each line, shadowing
• Slower speed, jump line by line

★ REMEMBER WHAT YOU LEARN
• Save a word with its sentence and the exact moment in the video
• Spaced review (1, 2, 4, 7, 15, 30, 60 days) that replays the video moment
• Gap-fill, dictation and der·die·das practice, daily streak and reminder
• Add more example sentences from ChatGPT, Grok, DWDS, Cambridge or dict.cc in one tap
• Save a clip of the scene with the subtitle burnt in

★ SYNC WITH YOUR PC
• Optional sync of your words, sentences, progress and subtitles with SubPlayer for Windows, through a private gist in your own GitHub account

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Your videos never leave your phone.

SUBSCRIPTION
SubPlayer Pro is a yearly subscription with a 10-day free trial for new customers. After the trial, the yearly price shown in Google Play is charged, and the subscription renews every year until you cancel. Cancel any time in Google Play ▸ Payments & subscriptions; if you cancel during the trial, you pay nothing.

Dictionary data: Wiktionary via kaikki.org, CC BY-SA 4.0. Speech recognition: Whisper (OpenAI, MIT licence) via whisper.cpp.
```
