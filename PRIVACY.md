## The short version

SubPlayer has **no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking**. The developer runs **no server** and
receives **none of your data**. Your videos, subtitles, saved words and review progress stay on your device.
Some optional features send data directly from your device to a service you choose. Each is described below.

## What stays on your device

- The videos you open. SubPlayer only reads them; they are never uploaded.
- Subtitles made on your device (offline speech recognition), saved words and phrases, added example sentences,
  review and practice progress, settings.
- Your API keys and GitHub token, if you enter them. They are stored only in the app's private storage.

Uninstalling the app (or "Clear storage" in Android settings) deletes all of this.

## What leaves your device, and only when you use the feature

| Feature | What is sent | Sent to | Their privacy policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Downloads** of dictionaries and speech models | A normal file request (your IP address) | GitHub (Microsoft) | https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement |
| **Online subtitles** (optional, you add your own key) | The video's **audio** | Groq or OpenAI, whichever you choose | https://groq.com/privacy-policy · https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy |
| **Translate sentence / phrase** | The sentence or phrase | Google Translate, or MyMemory if Google is unreachable | https://policies.google.com/privacy · https://mymemory.translated.net/doc/en/tos.php |
| **Real recordings** in the dictionary | A file request | Wikimedia Commons | https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy |
| **Sync** (optional, you add your own GitHub token) | Your saved words, sentences, review progress, streak and subtitles | A **secret gist in your own GitHub account** | GitHub, as above |
| **Learn more** buttons (ChatGPT, Grok, DWDS, Cambridge, dict.cc) | The word and its sentence | The app or website you tap, opened by Android | That service's own policy |
| **Video links** (direct-download version only) | The link you paste, then a normal file request | The website of that link | That website's own policy |
| **Subscription** (Google Play version) | Purchase handled entirely by Google Play | Google | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| **Subscription** (Cafe Bazaar / Myket versions) | Purchase handled by the store app on your phone | Cafe Bazaar (Hezardastan) or Myket | https://cafebazaar.ir/privacy · https://myket.ir/privacy |
| **"Install Myket" link** (Myket version, only if Myket is missing and you tap Install) | Phone model, Android version, advertising ID, app name — added by Myket's billing library to its install link | Myket | https://myket.ir/privacy |

The developer never sees any of this data. SubPlayer sends nothing in the background and nothing for any other purpose.
All connections use HTTPS encryption.

## Notifications

Review reminders are created on your device. No push service is used.

## Children

SubPlayer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You delete your
data in the app itself (delete words, stop syncing, uninstall) and, for synced data, by deleting the gist
"SubPlayer sync (private)" in your GitHub account. For data a third-party service holds (for example Groq, OpenAI or
Google), contact that service. You may complain to a data-protection authority, for example the authority of your federal
state in Germany.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date.
